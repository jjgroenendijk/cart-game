## Scope

Fourth biome on the 025 framework. A frozen tundra world: flat snowy plains
with low drifts, pines, ice rocks, snow bushes, blizzards. Registered as pure
data into `BIOMES` + the flora registry; no engine change to
heightAt/colorAt/normalAt. Distinct from temperate/desert/alpine: tundra is
FLAT + COLD (low-amplitude snow field, pale snow palette, frozen ground in
the low drifts, sparse snow-laden pines + pale ice rocks, cold overcast
sky/fog, frozen pale water).

## Notes

- Repo rule forbids committed media, so no PNG is committed; the live readout
  block above substitutes for the F3 screenshot once captured.
- Flora counts are PER-CHUNK (the dressing framework places `count` per
  streamed chunk). The 027 plan listed world-total-scale numbers
  (pine ~70, ice-rock ~60, snow-bush ~150); recalibrated to per-chunk values
  (pine 3, iceRock 2, snowBush 20) comparable to temperate
  (tree 2/rock 1/bush 3/flower 23/grass 47), desert
  (cactus 2/sandRock 2/yucca 5/dryShrub 30), and alpine
  (alpinePine 3/screeRock 2/lichenBush 25). Same recalibration precedent as
  026/028.
- Terrain tuning (plan-flagged risks): noiseAmp 3 (low flat relief w/ drifts)
  - noiseFreq 0.014 (moderate). sandLevel -1 (low) so the frozen-ground/ice
    sand band reads in the low drift pockets of the flat plain rather than only
    in deep pits; rockSlope 0.9 (default) shows rock only on the rare steeper
    bumps. waterLevel -4 (frozen pale lakes, decoupled from sandLevel via the
    025 fix) sits below the drift band so water stays in the lowest pockets.
    These are pure data values; revisit against a live frame if the snow band
    reads too uniform or the frozen ground is too sparse/broad.
- Pine is visually distinct from alpine's alpinePine: shorter stocky trunk
  (trunkH 5 vs 8), fewer broader tiers ([3,4] vs [4,5]), pale blue-green
  foliage + a pale snow-cap on the topmost tier, vs alpine's tall dark spire.
