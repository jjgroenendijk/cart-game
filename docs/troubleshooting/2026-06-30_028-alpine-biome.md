## Scope

Third biome on the 025 framework. An alpine world: high snowy peaks, pine
forests, granite cliffs, thin cold air. Registered as pure data into
`BIOMES` + the flora registry; no engine change to heightAt/colorAt. Distinct
from desert (026, flat dunes) and temperate: alpine is VERTICAL
(high-amplitude peaks + cliffs, granite rock on steep slopes, spire pines,
cold pale mountain lakes).

## Notes

- Repo rule forbids committed media, so no PNG is committed; the live readout
  above substitutes for the F3 screenshot (numbers captured from the running
  build via the dev console).
- Flora counts are PER-CHUNK (the dressing framework places `count` per
  streamed chunk). The 028 plan listed world-total-scale numbers; they were
  recalibrated to per-chunk values (alpinePine 3, screeRock 2, lichenBush 25)
  comparable to temperate (tree 2/rock 1/bush 3/flower 23/grass 47) and desert

  and perf in budget. Same recalibration precedent as 026.
- noiseAmp 14 (2x temperate's 7) + rockSlope 0.7 (below the 0.9 default) so
  granite rock shows through on the steep cliff faces the high amplitude
  produces. waterLevel -5 (decoupled from sandLevel -3 via the 025 fix) gives
  cold pale lakes only in the deepest valleys; peaks read well above the fog
  band. triangle count (278725) is higher than desert (212967) as expected for
  high-amplitude mountains + pine forest; still well within budget.
